# Collaborate on Still

Still serves one need: the mind at peace. This page lists concrete, scoped ways an organization can work with the project. Nothing on this page implies an existing partnership; collaborations are invitations until they actually happen, and no partner is named publicly without their written agreement.

One boundary stands above every task here: Still is a quiet practice tool, not medical care. Collaborations never turn it into a clinical product, and no partner is ever described as endorsing a health claim, because this project makes none.

## Who this is for

Libraries, schools, shelters, community centers, and workplaces that want a calm, private page people can open without an account, a download, or a signal.

## Sized tasks

1. **Put Still on a public screen or a printed card.** Run the page on a library or center computer, or print the practice as a card. Tell us where it lives and what people said about the words. Wording feedback is the most useful thing you can send.
2. **Review the words.** If your community includes plain language readers, counselors, or translators, review the page text for clarity and tone. Suggested edits arrive by issue or pull request and are credited by name, stage name, or anonymously, as the reviewer chooses.
3. **Adopt Still for your own community.** Fork the repository, translate or adapt the words under the same rules (no medical claims, crisis lines correct for your country, writing stays on the device), and tell us. Your fork is linked here.

## What you get

A public, dated record of the adoption or review, committed in this repository with your organization's role stated as you approve it.

## Start

Open an issue in this repository. Small and specific beats grand and vague.
