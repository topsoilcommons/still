# Agents working in this repo

These rules bind every contributor here, human or agent. This page sits next to people on hard days. The rules are strict on purpose.

## No medical claims, ever

- No diagnosis. No treatment claims. No efficacy claims. No wording that implies this tool improves, reduces, or helps any condition.
- The check in lines are supportive and plain. They must never sound like therapy, assessment, or advice about a person's health.
- The page states plainly: this is a quiet tool, not medical care. That sentence does not get edited away.
- The crisis block carries only the lines and numbers their operators publish: call or text 988, chat at 988lifeline.org, text HOME to 741741. Recheck them against the operator and FCC or SAMHSA pages before changing a word.

## Evidence first

- Numbers only from logged runs. The pacer drift figure in the README comes from test/test_pacer.js, and its output is committed at test/pacer_result.json. Rerun the test before restating the number.
- The grounding practice keeps its public source named on the page. Any new practice needs a named public source too, described in our own words.

## Privacy is the product

- No network calls. No analytics, no fonts, no external assets, no server.
- The writing space uses localStorage on the visitor's device only. A change that sends writing anywhere, in any form, does not merge.

## Style

- Plain short sentences. Visual first, minimal text. Calm before clever.
- No em dashes or en dashes in any copy.
- One improvement per pull request, with the run or check attached.
