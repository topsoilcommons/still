Check in

Pick the word closest to how you feel right now.

One grounding practice: 5-4-3-2-1

A way to come back to the room you are in, one sense at a time.

  1. Name five things you can see.
  2. Name four things you can touch.
  3. Name three things you can hear.
  4. Name two things you can smell.
  5. Name one thing you can taste.

Source: University of Rochester Medical Center, Behavioral Health Partners blog. The steps above are written in our own words.
